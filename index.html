I study how anatomical structures are organized and how they change, connecting clinical and applied anatomy with imaging and computational methods — micro-CT, ultrasonography, statistical shape modeling, and structure-tensor analysis. My current work spans the fascial architecture of the hand and age-related remodeling of dental hard tissues. Coming from a physical therapy background, I care about how anatomy translates into practice.
