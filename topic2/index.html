Directional Remodeling of the Dental Pulp Chamber with Aging

Revealed by Statistical Shape Modeling

Oral presentation · 22nd Congress of the IFAA (IFAA 2026) · Melbourne, Australia · 13–16 Aug 2026

Mandibular canine · Micro-CT · ShapeWorks · N = 20 (pilot)

This talk examines how the pulp chamber of the mandibular canine changes shape with age. Using micro-CT imaging and statistical shape modeling (ShapeWorks) on a pilot cohort, it characterizes the direction of age-related remodeling rather than volume change alone.
