Structured Continuity of the Dorsal Hand Fascia

Quantitative directional decomposition of collagen orientation

Poster P0063 · 22nd Congress of the IFAA (IFAA 2026) · Melbourne, Australia · 13–16 Aug 2026

Goun Jung, Jong Seo Kim, Kyung-Seok Hu, Hee-Jin Kim

The dorsal hand fascia is neither layered nor formless, but a structured continuity — persistent tangential sheets interwoven with perpendicular bridges, forming a proximal-to-distal mobility gradient.
